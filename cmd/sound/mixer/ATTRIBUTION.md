# Demo clip attribution

This demo plays two Clips, and they come from two places for a reason worth
stating: one of them is short enough to embed and one of them is the whole point
of not embedding anything.

## `pianoroll.ogg` — the one-shot, embedded

`pianoroll.ogg` is the first 48704 sample frames — 1.104399 seconds, stereo
44.1 kHz — of
[Pleasant Moments Piano Roll](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pleasant_Moments_Piano_Roll.ogg)
from Wikimedia Commons, which is **public domain**. It was cut at an Ogg page
boundary and that page was marked end-of-stream with its CRC recomputed; nothing
was re-encoded, so the stream is Ogg Vorbis exactly as published. It is the same
cut `cmd/sound/orbit`, `cmd/sound/buses`, `cmd/sound/crowd` and
`cmd/sound/emitters` embed, and the same one the engine's own `nosound` tests
use.

It sits here rather than in `assets/` for the reason it sits beside those four:
`assets/` is generated by `cmd/prepare-assets` from a manifest of `.glb` models,
and one embedded second of audio should not have to join it. Decoded it is about
190 KiB, well under the 512 KiB `DecodedClipLimit`, so it is decoded once at
load and every Voice playing it shares those samples.

## The music — not here, and not embedded

The music is `assets/music/pleasant-moments.ogg`, the same recording uncut:
176.181 seconds, 2.7 MiB encoded and 59.3 MiB decoded. It is vendored under
`assets/` with its own credits in
[`assets/music/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../../../assets/music/ATTRIBUTION.md), and the
demo names it by path through the `assets` mount rather than embedding it.

Embedding it would put 2.7 MiB in the binary and 59.3 MiB in the process the
moment anything played it, which is the argument the streamed tier exists for —
so a demo about that tier embedding it anyway would be arguing against itself.

The public-domain recording was chosen over the rest of the
[#304](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/304) prototype kit, four of whose
five clips are CC BY-SA, so that nothing here carries a share-alike obligation.
